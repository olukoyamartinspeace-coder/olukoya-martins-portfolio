Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

This page explains how Olukoya Martins ("I", "me") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit this portfolio site or use the contact form.

1. Information I collect

When you submit the contact form on this site, I receive the details you enter: your name, your email address, the project type, an optional budget figure, and your message. I do not ask for — and you should not send — payment details, passwords, or other sensitive personal data through the form.

Like most websites, the hosting and infrastructure services that serve this site may automatically log basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and reliability purposes.

2. How I use your information

I use the information you provide for one purpose: to read your inquiry and respond to it. If we go on to work together, the same details are used to deliver the service, send invoices, and communicate about the project. I do not use your data for marketing, advertising, or any automated decision-making.

3. Cookies and analytics

This site is a simple static portfolio. It does not set advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not use third-party analytics to profile visitors. The Google Fonts service may load fonts from Google's servers; Google's own privacy terms apply to that connection. When you use the contact form, the form delivery service processes your submission purely to route it to my email inbox.

4. Sharing your information

I do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. It is only shared with the third-party services required to operate this site and respond to you — for example, the email and form-delivery providers that carry your message to my inbox. These providers process data under their own privacy policies and only as needed to perform that function.

5. Data retention

Inquiries are kept for as long as they are useful — typically as long as a conversation or project is active, plus a reasonable period for records and follow-up. If you would like an inquiry removed sooner, just ask and I'll delete it.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information I hold about you at any time. You may also ask me to stop processing it. To make any of these requests, email me at the address below.

7. Security

I use reasonable technical and organisational measures to keep your information safe — including secure, password-protected email and storage. No method of transmission over the internet is fully secure, so while I work to protect your data, I cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be called out on the main site. Continued use of the site after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions, concerns, or data requests can be sent to peacedamola534@gmail.com.
